**Where you stand**
* Aapke bhai custody mein hain aur kal unki court mein peshic hai, isliye aapko turant vakil (lawyer) ki zaroorat hai.
* Aapko free legal aid ya district court se panel lawyer ke liye [[RES-NALSA]] madad kar sakta hai.

**What you can do**
1. District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) se sampark karein jo court ke paas hota hai, ya free legal aid ke liye [[RES-NALSA]] (15100) par call karein.
2. Kal court mein peshi ke samay ek criminal lawyer ko sath rakhein jo bail application file kar sakein.
3. Agar aapko koi emergency madad chahiye toh [[RES-112]] par call karein.
